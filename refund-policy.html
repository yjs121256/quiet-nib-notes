Refund policy

This refund policy applies to paid Catalog Sentry Local plans sold by 윤주컴퍼니 (Yunju Company), Gwangju, Korea. The free command-line tool and the free editorial pages on this site are not sold, so no refund arises for them.

No paid plan is for sale on this site yet. The policy below is the policy that will apply when a paid plan is sold through Paddle. Contact: yjs121256@gmail.com.

Who handles the refund

Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com. Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record for all our orders. Paddle provides all customer service inquiries and handles returns.

Paddle’s own buyer refund policy also applies, and where local law gives you a stronger right, that right is not taken away (Paddle Refund Policy).

30-day money-back for paid subscriptions

If you buy a paid Catalog Sentry Local subscription, you may request a full refund within 30 days of the transaction date, for any reason. This follows Paddle’s recommended practice of at least a 30-day money-back guarantee (Paddle customer handbook).

Korea: 7-day cancellation right

If you are a consumer in South Korea, you also have a 7-day unconditional right to cancel a digital content or service contract after delivery and receive a full refund, as described in Paddle’s country-specific rules (Paddle Refund Policy, South Korea). That statutory right is in addition to the 30-day policy above, and the more favourable right applies.

How to request a refund

Email yjs121256@gmail.com or contact Paddle through the receipt they send you. Please include the order reference from your receipt. We will not ask you to invent a reason, and we will not publish your request.

After a refund

Access to a paid plan ends when the refund is completed. The free command-line tool remains available.
