About

An independent research-based site

Quiet Nib Notes is an independent, research-based site about everyday care and troubleshooting for people who own a cartridge or converter fountain pen.

It is not a manufacturer, retailer, authorised service centre, or repair workshop. The publisher does not claim formal pen-repair training, trade certification, laboratory testing, or years of workshop experience. Nothing on this site is a substitute for the instructions that came with your pen, or for help from the maker or a qualified repairer.

How the guides are written

Each article starts from a common question (a skipped line, a converter that will not fill, a first setup) and works through checks in order, from the least invasive to the point where you should stop. Statements about what to do — and when not to do it — are drawn from publicly available manufacturer and specialist-retailer guidance, which is linked in the article. Where sources disagree, the article says so rather than picking a winner quietly.

The site does not present stock photographs, unpaid testimonials, or invented case studies as first-hand tests. If original photographs are added later, they will be photographs actually taken for that purpose.

Who to contact

Questions about the site itself can be sent to yjs121256@gmail.com. Please do not send a pen or ask for a personal diagnosis of a damaged nib or an unusual filling system; we cannot take that on.

Other pages here

Alongside the fountain-pen guides, this same site hosts a web archive of Merchant Evidence Brief (a short, primary-source newsletter about product data and crawlers), the privacy policy for the Product Evidence Lens browser extension, and a product page for Catalog Sentry Local, a local CSV preflight tool. Those projects are also independent and research-based. They are not client work and are not presented as such.
