Privacy policy

This policy is for the Quiet Nib Notes website (including the newsletter archive on this domain). The Product Evidence Lens browser extension has its own policy.

The website does not have accounts, comments, a newsletter signup form, analytics scripts, advertising pixels, or cookies that we set. It is a static site. We do not run a server-side application that logs visitors for marketing, and we do not use Google Analytics or a similar third-party analytics product on these pages.

What we may receive anyway

  • Email. If you write to yjs121256@gmail.com, we receive whatever you send, and the usual records that an email provider keeps. We use that correspondence to reply. We do not sell it.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect standard technical request data (such as IP address, user agent, and the page requested) as part of operating that service. Their terms and privacy policy apply to that processing: GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Links to other sites. Articles cite manufacturer and retailer pages. Those sites have their own policies. We are not responsible for them.
  • Payments (if a paid plan is purchased). If you buy a paid Catalog Sentry Local plan, the order is processed by our online reseller Paddle.com, which is the merchant of record. Paddle collects the payment and billing information needed to complete that order. Paddle’s own privacy policy applies to that processing: Paddle Privacy Policy. We do not receive or store your card details. No paid checkout is live on this site until a Paddle account is verified and a checkout is actually connected.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal information, we do not use the site to build an advertising profile, and we do not require you to create an account to read the guides.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date below. Questions: yjs121256@gmail.com.
